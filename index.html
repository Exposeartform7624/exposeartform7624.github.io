🌟 What Is File-Organiser?

Does your Downloads folder look like a digital landfill? Files scattered everywhere—pictures mixed with PDFs, installer files next to old presentations. You know exactly what I'm talking about.

File-Organiser is a friendly little background app for Windows that pays attention to your folders and does the tidying up while you don't even notice it. You tell it one time: "Send photos to Pictures, keep documents in Documents, put zips in the Archive folder." And it does exactly that—automatically, for every new file that appears.

No more dragging and dropping. No more huge piles of random stuff. Just clear, flexible organization that obeys your rules.

Minimum to run comfortably:

🔒 Safety & Security

We take file safety seriously. File-Organiser only moves files according to the rules you set. It won't delete anything on its own. You always remain in full control and can tweak or disable any rule in one click.

Your data stays entirely on your computer. No cloud sync, no hidden uploads, no sign-up needed.

🙋 Frequently Asked Questions

🔹 Does it work on macOS/Linux?

Cross-platform support is planned, but the current stable release runs on Windows.

🔹 Can I undo a move if I change my mind?

Yes! Just go to the folder and move manually—your rules can be edited at any moment, but there's no auto-undo yet. We recommend trying on a test folder first.
